About the Creators

Meet Hannah and Claire.

Two friends, one sleepover, a pair of rollerblades, and a very important question: why does learning to skate have to be so hard on your butt?

The origin story

From second grade friends to safety inventors.

1

Sunset Elementary

Hannah and Claire met in 2nd Grade at Sunset Elementary School. They became great friends fast, the kind who laugh at the same jokes and make every game a team sport.

2

The sleepover discovery

At a sleepover party, they spotted rollerblades in the garage and decided they had to try them. The idea was brilliant. The first few landings were not.

3

The Butt Pad is born

After many falls, Hannah and Claire got better at skating. They also realized kids needed a funny, comfy way to protect their butts, so they invented the Butt Pad.

Their invention rule is simple: if learning something new comes with a lot of falling down, make the falling part softer and the getting-back-up part funnier.
